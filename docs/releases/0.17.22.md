# Iron & Nouveau 0.17.22

- Allow Iron projectile replacement glyphs at the beginning of form-free inscription templates.
- Restore compatibility with Ars tools that supply their own cast method, including the wand's native Projectile and Accelerate prefix.
- Preserve duplicate-payload, disabled-glyph, actual cast-method, and modifier restrictions after inscription.

Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge and Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge.
