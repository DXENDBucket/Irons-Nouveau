# Iron & Nouveau 0.17.23

- Allow surviving native Iron projectiles to continue their Ars recipe again after another successful hit on the same target.
- Remove the extra lifetime target set, its 128-target cap, and the one-block-impact lifetime limit. Native hit cooldowns, piercing limits, permissions and trigger costs remain unchanged.
- Failed damage still does not continue the recipe. Multiple successful damage/status notifications inside one complex native callback still continue once per target; a later callback has a fresh scope.

Available for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge and Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge.
