- Add Undead Rift, Fiery Dagger, Dagger Zone, Ring of Fire and Expulsion from Iron's native Boss/item abilities.
- Keep native summons, rising blades, fire fields, multipart walls, sounds and projectile visuals. Standard projectiles use the dagger's native preparation and launch; other supported carriers retain Ars trajectories.
- Add real material-based Scribe's Table recipes, localized descriptions, native-resource icons and Conflux/Instinct hints for all five glyphs.
- Enforce the rift's shared twelve-summon limit, real ownership and cleanup. Sustained fire fields pay during successful damage windows and stop on insufficient mana.
- Integrate Iron casting speed/cooldown attributes and the existing Conflux casting, resource and damage APIs. Ars Instinct remains optional.

Forge 1.20.1 does not register an Expulsion Ring; its glyph recipe uses a Cooldown Ring and a Feather instead. Other glyph recipes are shared. No copied Iron assets or hidden Boss executor.
